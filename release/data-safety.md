# Google Play Data safety draft

Declaration: No data collected or shared.

Basis: the Android wrapper requests no permissions, includes no network permission, and loads only bundled local assets. Ingredient data, favorites, and menu history remain in local WebView storage. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking, remote APIs, or third-party data recipients.

Owner review required: re-confirm this declaration against the final generated AAB, all included dependencies, and the current Google Play Data safety questionnaire immediately before submission. Do not answer age, content-rating, ads, or privacy-policy URL questions from this draft alone.
