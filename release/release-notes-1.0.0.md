# Fridge Menu 1.0.0 release notes

Initial Google Play submission candidate.

- Create three deterministic, use-first meal directions from three to eight ingredients.
- Match directions to the actual ingredients: rice and egg surface a fried rice, kimchi surfaces a stew, noodles surface a noodle bowl, vegetables surface a fresh salad, with distinct titles and methods for every menu.
- Understand English and Korean ingredient names, and ship the full UI in English and Korean with automatic device-language detection plus an in-app toggle.
- Accept ingredients without an expiry date; dated ingredients still lead in use-first order.
- Keep ingredients, favorites, and menu history on-device only.
- Exclude expired ingredients from meal directions while keeping them visible for removal.
- Work without accounts, analytics, tracking, ad SDKs, remote APIs, or network permissions.

Known boundary: this candidate provides cooking prompts only. It does not provide recipes, nutrition, allergy, medical, spoilage, or food-safety advice.
