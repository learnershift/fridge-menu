# Fridge Menu privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-08-01

Fridge Menu has no account, analytics, advertising SDK, tracking, or remote API. The app stores your ingredient list, favorites, and recent menus only in the browser or Android WebView storage on your device. This information is not sent to us or shared with third parties.

You can clear the ingredient list from the app. Clearing browser or app storage also removes the locally stored data. We do not receive, retain, sell, or disclose personal information because the app does not transmit it.

This policy must be published by the owner at a stable public HTTPS URL before Google Play submission. For privacy questions, the owner must provide a monitored support contact in the Play Console listing.
